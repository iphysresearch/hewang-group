I am always happy to hear from students who want to work on gravitational-wave data analysis, machine learning, or AI for science.

## What the work looks like

Most projects start with a concrete dataset and a question that can be settled by a test. That means writing code, reading waveforms, and being willing to find out that an idea does not work. The group is small, so you will get direct feedback rather than a queue.

## Before you write

It helps if you can tell me, in a few sentences, which of the [research directions](/research/) you are drawn to and what you have already tried — a course project, a reproduction, a notebook someone else gave up on.

## Where to write

Email He Wang at [wanghe@ucas.ac.cn](mailto:wanghe@ucas.ac.cn) with a short note and, if you have one, a link to something you built.
